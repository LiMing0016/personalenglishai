# Grammar Taxonomy

Use these labels for common learner grammar issues.

## Issue Types

- `tense`: wrong or inconsistent verb tense.
- `subject_verb_agreement`: subject and verb do not agree.
- `word_order`: unnatural or incorrect word order.
- `article`: missing, unnecessary, or incorrect `a`, `an`, or `the`.
- `preposition`: incorrect or missing preposition.
- `word_form`: wrong part of speech or inflection.
- `collocation`: unnatural word combination.
- `sentence_fragment`: incomplete sentence.
- `run_on_sentence`: two or more clauses incorrectly joined.
- `punctuation`: punctuation affects correctness or readability.
- `spelling`: misspelled word.
- `other`: clear grammar issue that does not fit the labels above.

## Non-Error Labels

Use these labels only when the caller needs to distinguish grammar errors from improvement suggestions.

- `style_suggestion`: grammar is acceptable, but expression could be more natural, formal, concise, or writing-appropriate.
- `not_grammar_error`: the text is grammatically acceptable; no grammar correction is required.

## Severity

- `low`: minor issue; meaning is clear and writing quality is only slightly affected.
- `medium`: noticeable issue; sentence remains understandable but sounds incorrect or learner-like.
- `high`: issue blocks meaning, creates serious ambiguity, or would strongly affect writing evaluation.
